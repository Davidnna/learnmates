Frequently asked questions

Quick answers about using LearnMates AI and understanding its current limitations.

Can I explore the website without signing up?

Yes. The home, About, Features, How It Works, FAQ, and Privacy pages are public. An account is needed for personal notes, chat history, quizzes, and progress records.

Is LearnMates AI free to use?

Creating an account may be free during the pilot, but the service relies on hosting and AI providers whose free plans, quotas, and availability can change. The site does not guarantee unlimited AI usage.

Why might an AI answer fail?

The provider may be temporarily busy, the model may be unavailable to the configured API key, or a quota/rate limit may have been reached. If a request fails, try again later. A working website does not necessarily mean the external AI service is available.

Which files can I upload?

The current application accepts PDF and TXT files up to 5 MB. Text-based PDFs work best. Scanned PDFs that contain only page images may not yield extractable text because OCR is not included.

Does the AI only use my notes?

When you ask a question with relevant notes selected, the app can retrieve passages from those notes and include them in the prompt. Answers can still include mistakes and should be checked against the original material.

Are my quiz scores an official grade?

No. Scores are for personal practice and feedback inside the application. They are not official course or examination results.

What information does the app save?

The application stores account details, password hashes, uploaded-note text, chat messages, and quiz attempts in its configured database. The original uploaded file storage on the current free hosting setup may not persist across restarts or redeploys. See the Privacy page before uploading sensitive information.

What should I do if something looks wrong?

Refresh the page and retry once. For repeated AI failures, the site operator should review the hosting logs and AI provider quota/model settings. Do not send API keys or passwords when reporting an issue.
