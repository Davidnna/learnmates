Frequently asked questions

Quick answers about using LearnMates AI and understanding its current limitations.

Can I explore the website without signing up?

Yes. The home, About, Features, How It Works, FAQ, and Privacy pages are public. An account is needed for personal notes, chat history, quizzes, and progress records.

Is LearnMates AI free to use?

LearnMates AI may be available at no cost during the current access period. Availability and usage limits may change over time.

Why might an AI answer fail?

The AI service may be temporarily busy or unavailable. Wait a moment and try again. If the problem continues, contact the site administrator.

Which files can I upload?

You can upload PDF and TXT files up to 5 MB. Text-based PDFs work best. Scanned PDFs that contain only page images may not yield extractable text because they contain images instead of selectable text.

Does the AI only use my notes?

When you ask a question with relevant notes selected, the app can retrieve passages from those notes and include them in the prompt. Answers can still include mistakes and should be checked against the original material.

Are my quiz scores an official grade?

No. Scores are for personal practice and feedback inside the application. They are not official course or examination results.

What information does the app save?

LearnMates AI stores account details, protected password information, extracted note text, chat messages, and quiz attempts. Original uploaded files may not always remain available after service maintenance or updates. See the Privacy page before uploading sensitive information.

What should I do if something looks wrong?

Refresh the page and try again. If the problem continues, contact the site administrator and describe what you were doing. Never share your password or other private account details.

Still deciding?

Review the feature guide or create an account to explore the personal tools.
