GETTING STARTED

From study material to a focused revision session.

LearnMeet AI is organised around a simple cycle: add learning material, ask or practise, then review what you learned.

  1. 1

    Create an account

    Register to open your personal learning space. Your notes, conversations, and quiz attempts are associated with your account.

  2. 2

    Add study material or choose a topic

    Upload a text-based PDF or TXT file, or paste content into a study tool. Clear, selectable text works best; scanned PDFs may not provide readable text.

  3. 3

    Ask, summarise, or practise

    Use the AI Tutor for explanations, Study Tools for revision, and Practice Quiz to test your understanding. AI features may occasionally be unavailable. If a response fails, wait a moment and try again.

  4. 4

    Review feedback and revisit weak areas

    Check answer explanations and recorded scores. Use the results as study guidance, not as a formal assessment or guarantee of mastery.
