ABOUT THE PROJECT

A study companion built around how students learn.

LearnMates AI is a web-based student learning assistance system designed to bring study support, personal notes, revision activities, and quiz feedback together in one place.

Get started →Explore features

What it aims to help with

Students often move between lecture notes, revision questions, and separate tools when preparing for classes or assessments. LearnMates AI aims to make common study activities easier to access from a single account.

  • Ask an AI tutor for explanations and examples when the AI service is available.
  • Keep PDF and text study notes in a personal library.
  • Use summaries, flashcards, and practice quizzes to support revision.
  • Review quiz results and simple progress indicators over time.

Designed to support—not replace—learning

LearnMates AI is a study aid, not an official source of course requirements or a substitute for lecturers, textbooks, or independent reasoning. AI-generated answers may be incomplete or incorrect. Students should check important claims against trusted materials.

Current scope

LearnMates AI supports accounts, note uploads, AI-assisted study workflows, quizzes, score history, and basic progress summaries. Some AI features may occasionally be unavailable.
