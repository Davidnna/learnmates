PRIVACY & DATA

Understand what LearnMeets AI handles.

This notice explains what information LearnMeets AI stores and how it is used. Please read it before uploading study materials.

Information the app stores

When you register and use personal tools, LearnMeets AI stores your name, email address, a protected password representation (not your plain-text password), extracted note text, chat messages, and quiz attempts and scores.

Use of AI features

When you use an AI feature, your question and the information needed to answer it may be sent to an external AI service. If you choose to use your notes, relevant passages may be included. That service processes information under its own privacy terms.

Files and hosting

LearnMeets AI accepts PDF and TXT uploads up to 5 MB. Text extracted from your files is saved so it can be used by study tools. Original file availability may be affected by service maintenance or redeployment. Hosting and database providers process data under their own terms.

Practical precautions

  • Do not upload passwords, financial information, health records, confidential assessments, or other sensitive personal information.
  • Use only material you are allowed to upload and share with an external AI service.
  • Keep your password private and sign out on shared devices.
  • Remember that AI-generated output may be inaccurate.

Retention and requests

If you need help with your account or stored information, contact the service administrator. Do not upload confidential or sensitive records.

Privacy by caution

Use LearnMeets AI with ordinary study materials rather than confidential or sensitive records.
