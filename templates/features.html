FEATURE GUIDE

Tools for understanding, practice, and review.

Here is what each part of LearnMates AI is designed to do. An account is required to use the personal learning tools.

✦

AI Tutor

Ask questions about a concept and request an explanation, an example, or a clearer breakdown. Responses depend on the configured AI provider and available quota.

AI-assisted
▤

Personal Notes

Upload PDF or TXT study material. The app extracts readable text and can retrieve relevant passages to help ground a response in your notes.

PDF / TXT · up to 5 MB
✎

Study Tools

Work with summaries and revision flashcards based on pasted or selected learning material. Quality depends on the readability and completeness of the source.

Revision support
✓

Practice Quizzes

Answer multiple-choice questions, check your score, and review explanations. When notes are selected, the app validates source evidence for supported quiz items.

Practice and feedback
↗

Progress Overview

See recorded quiz attempts, score trends, and simple study recommendations derived from past results.

Based on recorded attempts
🔒

Personal account

Sign in to access your saved chat history, notes, and quiz records. Keep your password private and avoid uploading confidential or highly sensitive material.

Sign-in required
